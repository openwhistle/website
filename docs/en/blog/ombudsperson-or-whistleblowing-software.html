Ombudsperson or whistleblowing software? What the HinSchG really requires

Ombudsperson or whistleblowing software is the wrong question: software is the channel, the reporting office is people. Who does which task.

27 October 2026 Updated 27 October 2026 4 min read OpenWhistle Contributors

The German Whistleblower Protection Act (HinSchG) requires neither an ombudsperson nor any particular software [1]. It requires an internal reporting office with reporting channels (§ 12, § 16). The office can be your own employees or a third party, such as an ombudsperson (§ 14 Abs. 1). Whistleblowing software is the channel through which reports reach it.

Not legal advice

This article gives general information. It reflects the HinSchG as amended on 25 September 2026 [1].
